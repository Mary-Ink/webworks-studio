# Week 6 — UX Evaluation Brief
## Hill Country Trail Guide

**Primary User:** Maya Torres  
**Primary Task:** Choose a beginner-appropriate Saturday hike that can be completed in about three hours or less.

---

## 1. Task Walkthrough
Briefly describe what Maya would try to do first, what she would look for, and where she might hesitate.

Maya would want to do first is to look for an easy trail that fits her time and experience. She can look for the trail distance, difficulty, and elevation, although some of the information she needs won't be easy to find. Comparing the trails would be more work to look when using moblie.

---

## 2. Five UX Findings


### Finding 1
**Observation:**  
The trail cards don't show the hiking time.

**Evidence:**  
The cards show the distance and elevation, although it doesn't show an estimated time for each card.

**User Impact:**  
Maya won't be able to figure out if the hike would be three hours or less.

**Principle:**  
Visibility for information

**Priority:** High

**Recommendation:**  
Show the real estimated time for each hike in each trail card.

### Finding 2
**Observation:**
The difficulty levels in the trail cards aren't explained well.

**Evidence:**
The trails are shown as "Easy", and "Moderate", but don't really explain more on what they mean.

**User Impact:**
Maya might accidentally choose a trail that might be too difficult instead of being easy.

**Principle:**
Plain language.

**Priority:** High


**Recommendation:**  
Explain the difficult levels in simple terms for those to understand.

### Finding 3
**Observation:**
The filter choices aren't very clear.

**Evidence:**
It shows "Easy", "Moderate", "Hard", "Under 5 miles", and "Dog friendly."

**User Impact:**
Maya might not know which options to choose that will help her find the right trail.

**Principle:**
Clear navigation

**Priority:** Medium


**Recommendation:**  
Make the trail filters easier to use, and clear to navagate.

### Finding 4
**Observation:**
Some important trail information is more lower down on the page.

**Evidence:**
The information on about parking, water, weather, and more, is farther down the page.

**User Impact:**
Maya could miss some information she would need before figuring out a trail.

**Principle:**
Information hierarchy

**Priority:** Medium


**Recommendation:**
Make important trail information that is more easier to find.

### Finding 5
**Observation:**
Comparing trails on the website is harder on the phone.

**Evidence:**
The trails are shown one after another, that way Maya has to scroll more to see all the different choices.

**User Impact:**
Maya has to spend more time comparing trails while time can be consuming scrolling so much.

**Principle:**
Mobile usuability.

**Priority:** Medium


**Recommendation:**
Make the more important trail information easier to compare on moblie.

---

## 3. Top Three Priorities
Identify the three findings that should move forward into Week 7.

1. **Showing Hike Time** Maya needs to know if the trails fit with at least three hours.
2. **Explain Difficult Levels** Maya is a beginner and needs to know what "easy" is actually like.
3. **Make Important Information Easier to Find** Maya needs the information like parking, water, and trail conditions before choosing a trail to hike.
